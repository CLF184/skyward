Skyward 0.2.5 for the Xiaomi 15 Pro (`haotian`), made on the stock recovery
`OS4.0.0.19.XOBCNXM`.

Image: `skyward-v0.2.5-haotian-OS4.0.0.19.XOBCNXM.img`. Check it against
`SHA256SUMS`, then, with the phone in fastboot mode on the slot it runs from:

```sh
sha256sum --check SHA256SUMS
fastboot flash recovery skyward-v0.2.5-haotian-OS4.0.0.19.XOBCNXM.img
fastboot reboot recovery
```

## 这次修的内容

- **双击亮屏改成设置里的开关，默认关闭。** 上一版为了保住双击唤醒，息屏时让面板
  保持 doze 而不是断电——代价是手机不休眠。现在这个取舍交给你：默认关，面板彻底
  断电、手机正常休眠；想要双击的在「设置 → 通用」里打开。
  - 打开时：面板保持低功耗待机，双击能点亮，但**本机不会休眠**，待机耗电略高。
  - 关闭时（默认）：面板完全断电，手机正常休眠，只能用电源键点亮。
  - 开关只在面板本身支持时出现。
- **README 同步上游。** 上游把功能介绍改成了「每个功能配一张图」，本分支跟进了
  同样的结构，同时保留本分支特有的内容（单机型、weaver 解密、亚像素渲染、双击、
  ADSP 重试、ART 清理、锁屏密码读取）。

## 怎么确认装上了

```sh
adb shell dmesg | grep -i "recovery up"   # 应出现 haotian 0.2.5
```

想确认双击开关生效：关着的时候熄屏后 `dmesg | grep -c FB_BLANK` 会随面板断电而
增加；打开后应一直保持 0。

## This release in English

**The two-tap wake is now a switch, off by default.** 0.2.4 kept the panel dozing
so the taps could be felt, and the price was that the phone never suspended. That
trade is now yours to make: off by default, the panel is taken down and the phone
sleeps; turned on in Settings → General, the panel dozes and the taps work. The
row only appears on a panel that can offer it.

**Both READMEs follow upstream's new shape**, where each feature carries a
picture, while keeping what is particular to this fork.

### Why the double tap costs power

Xiaomi's touch driver follows the display's blanking notices and suspends the
touch IC on them. From then on the touchscreen reports nothing at all while the
panel is dark, so a phone that must feel a tap has to keep its panel dozing — and
a dozing panel is a phone that never suspends. This panel exposes no wakeup
source to arm instead, which is why the gesture cannot be free here.
